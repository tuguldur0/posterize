Posterizer

Inspired by DitherBoy, posterizer is a dithering tool designed for lovers of pixel art aesthetics. Upload any image of your liking, apply nostalgic retro filters to them, choose any color palette that you like, and export your picture with cool pixelated effects.

Features:

1. Dithering-- Converts modern high resolution photos into low resolution, pixelated version of it.
2. Custom color palettes-- Switch between multiple color theme of your liking.
3. Instant preview-- Adjust the picture's scanline, the gap between them, the dark shade of it. Shift the chromatic abrevation as much as you like, find the perfect noise amplitude of the picture for yourself.
4. Export-- Download your pixel generated pictures instantly unlimitedly.
